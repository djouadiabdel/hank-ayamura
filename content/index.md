Hank Ayamura

Two bodies of work, both circling the same question.

## [Short Stories](her/)

Nineteen confessional pieces. A relationship, what it asked for, and what neither person had to give.

## [What These Hands Are For](ippo/)

A web novel. Forty-five chapters, the first arc. Boxing as the place a man goes to find out whether he exists outside of someone else's looking.
