---
title: The Lives of the Saints
---

Pieces assembled out of ninety days of one man's messages. Added as they are finished.

Each one takes a sentence he would defend and lets the record answer it. The sentences in them were typed by somebody. What surrounds them was built.

The titles are positions, not people. A man who lived seventeen years under his own father's staircase and was called the Man of God by a household that did not know him. An archangel standing at the end of things with a balance in his hand, putting a soul in one pan and what it did in the other. A priest on the other side of a grille who takes everyone's confession all night and has never once made his own. A man in a river who points at the one coming after him and does not follow him out of the water.

- [The Forerunner](the-forerunner) — *I bring people to something*
