---
title: "The Book of Elara"
---

## I

1 And it came to pass in those days that there was a city, and in the city a hall, and in the hall a woman that sat and gave judgement.

2 And they came in before her from the first hour even unto the ninth. The liars, and the takers, and they that had never in all their days been told nay.

3 And she heard them, every one. And she gave to each man his answer, and there was no answer among them that was not true.

4 And they went out from before her, and spake one to another, saying, She is a hard woman.

5 Howbeit there was not any among them that asked what a thing it costeth to be in the right from morning until night.

6 Now her anger was a just anger, and there was found none to bear it for her.

7 And she said, I answer them again, and afterward it repenteth me that ever I opened my mouth.

8 And in all that hall was there no man to say unto her, Thou wast not wrong.

9 Wherefore she took it up and bare it home, and laid it down, and in the morning she took it up again.

10 And she said, Yet a year. And at the end of the year I will go out from this land, and there shall not one of them follow after me.

## II

1 Now there was a saying concerning her, that her mouth was mingled with poison.

2 And whomsoever she kissed, he loved her all the days of his life, or else he died of it. And there was no third thing.

3 Wherefore she kissed no man.

4 And when any drew near unto her she was warm toward him, and gave him her whole heart for a season. And afterward she went her way as it pleased her, and his name was taken out of her, and she remembered him no more.

5 And they called it cruelty. And she suffered them to call it so.

6 And she said within herself, I am not as they are. Let them say what they will.

## III

1 And it came to pass afterward that there came a man and stood in the door, and he sat not down.

2 And they spake together, and the words of them were these.

> **ELARA**
> What wouldst thou have? They all stand where thou art,
> And all of them would have of me the same,
> A judgement or a kiss. Which is it, then?
>
> **HE**
> I have heard tell what thy mouth carries in it.
>
> **ELARA**
> 'Tis true. Then choose, and know the terms of it.
> Choose wrong, thou diest. Choose right, and thou art worse.
>
> **HE**
> To die upon a queen's hand is no death.
>
> **ELARA**
> So say they all. So said the last of them,
> And the last before him, and the one before,
> And every one of them lay down and died,
> Or lived, which is the same, and left me here
> Upon my feet. I am the one that stands.
> I have not sat since morning.
>
> **HE**
> &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;How was the day?
>
> **ELARA**
> What?
>
> **HE**
> How was the day? Was it a long one? Did
> The last of them cry out at thee? And hast
> Thou eaten anything since morning?

3 And she answered him not a word.

4 And she came down from off the seat, the which she had not done since the first hour, and she sat upon the step.

5 And the hall was empty. And she called not for the next.

6 Then rose she up, and took his face between her two hands, and kissed him. And there was no man that had asked it of her.

7 Now of the two things that are written concerning her kiss, it is not set down here which of them came upon him.

8 He asked it not.
