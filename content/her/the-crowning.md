---
title: "The Crowning"
---

It is dark and I have been walking. I want that written down first, because everything after it is going to argue with it.

There is a crown on me. It is heavier than it looks and it sits badly and there is nobody here to laugh at it.

I have not taken it off. I want to be exact, because it would come off. I have had my hands on it twice.

There is another one. Somewhere out from me, in the part of this I do not go into, there is a second crown and it is on somebody.

I have not seen the face.

I have the rest of her. I have the colour of the skin and the shape of the chest and the sweetness of the arms. I have a voice that is soft the way snow is soft in the hour before anybody has walked on it. I have a hug from a maiden who is in love.

I do not know who she is in love with. I have decided.

That is the part I would leave out if I were being careful.

There is a warmth ahead.

I noticed it on the first day, if these are days. It is not much. It is the difference between a room with somebody in it and a room without one, and I have spent enough time in both to be able to tell them apart in the dark.

So there is something ahead that is not nothing.

I walked toward it.

It is warmer than the cold is cold. I tested that. I stood and faced the other way and the back of me went cold at a rate the front of me did not, and I did it twice to be sure, and it held.

A thing that is warm is burning. Nothing is warm on its own.

So there is a fire.

Now I am going to lay it out properly, because I have been laying it out badly and arriving at the same place, and a man who gets the same answer every time ought to look at how he is asking.

A fire in a place like this is not for nothing. Nobody keeps one burning to no purpose. It is either for taking a thing up, or it is for making a thing ready.

If it were for taking up, it would have taken me. I have been walking at it for a long time and it has not come out to meet me once.

So it is the other one.

Which means this is not what I thought when I arrived.

Which means it ends.

Which means the second crown is somewhere on the far side of the heat, on somebody, and the face is turned this way or it is not, and I will find out, and the finding out is what the heat is for.

I have this down to about twenty minutes now. It used to take the whole of the dark.

I could go and look. She is behind me, or beside me, or she is near enough that if I stopped and said it, it would land.

I do not say it.

I have a word for that and I have looked the word up and it is a virtue. I checked which kind.

I stopped tonight.

I am putting this down plainly because I am not going to do anything with it.

I stopped and stood still and counted to sixty, and then I counted to sixty again, and I did not move my feet at all.

The heat was exactly where it had been.

Not nearer. Not further. The same on my face as it was on the first day, and the same on my hands, and I have walked a very long way.

There is an explanation for that.

I have four.

I am going to set them out tomorrow and put them in order and see which one holds, and I am good at this now, and one of them will hold.

The crown is heavier at the front. I have noticed that. It leans a person forward.

I have started walking again.
