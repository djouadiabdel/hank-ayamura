---
title: Seven Fires, Never Seven Hells
aliases:
  - crowning
---

A man goes down seven floors toward a fire. He is carrying seven things and an explanation for each of them, and he is telling all of it to the woman walking beside him, who has not said anything yet.

There is a gate after every floor. Each one takes the thing he would have chosen last.

<p class="sf-translit">Vaya'avod Ya'akov b'Rachel sheva shanim<br/>vayihyu v'einav k'yamim achadim b'ahavato otah</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">And he served seven years for her, and they seemed to him like a few days, because of his love for her.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">אָהַבְתִּי אֶת־אֲדֹנִי אֶת־אִשְׁתִּי וְאֶת־בָּנָי<br/>לֹא אֵצֵא חָפְשִׁי</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Ahavti et adoni, et ishti v'et banai<br/>lo etze chofshi</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">I love my master, and my wife, and my children. I will not go out free.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">הַבְרָכָה אַחַת הִוא־לְךָ אָבִי בָּרֲכֵנִי גַם־אָנִי אָבִי<br/>וַיִּשָּׂא עֵשָׂו קֹלוֹ וַיֵּבְךְּ</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Hav'rachah achat hi l'cha avi, barcheni gam ani avi<br/>vayisa Esav kolo vayevk</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">Do you have only one blessing, my father? Bless me too, my father. And he lifted his voice and wept.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">וַיְהִי בַבֹּקֶר וְהִנֵּה־הִוא לֵאָה<br/>הֲלֹא בְרָחֵל עָבַדְתִּי עִמָּךְ וְלָמָּה רִמִּיתָנִי</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Vay'hi vaboker v'hineh hi Leah<br/>halo v'Rachel avadeti imach v'lamah rimitani</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">And when the morning came, it was Leah. Did I not serve you for Rachel? Why then have you deceived me?</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">פִּתִּיתַנִי יְהוָה וָאֶפָּת<br/>חֲזַקְתַּנִי וַתּוּכָל</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Pititani Adonai va'epat<br/>chazaktani vatuchal</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">You deceived me, and I was deceived. You were stronger than me, and you prevailed.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">וְהֵם לֹא יָדְעוּ כִּי שֹׁמֵעַ יוֹסֵף<br/>כִּי הַמֵּלִיץ בֵּינֹתָם</p>
<p class="sf-translit">V'hem lo yad'u ki shomea Yosef<br/>ki hameilitz beinotam</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">And they did not know that he understood them, because there was an interpreter between them.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">כִּי רָאָה יְהוָה בְּעָנְיִי<br/>כִּי עַתָּה יֶאֱהָבַנִי אִישִׁי</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Ki ra'ah Adonai b'onyi<br/>ki atah ye'ehavani ishi</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">He has looked on my misery. Surely now my husband will love me.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">אֶדֶר הַיְקָר<br/>אֲשֶׁר יָקַרְתִּי מֵעֲלֵיהֶם</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Eder hay'kar<br/>asher yakarti mei'aleihem</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">The handsome price. The rate at which I was valued by them.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">וְעֵינֶיךָ רֹאוֹת וְכָלוֹת אֲלֵיהֶם כָּל־הַיּוֹם<br/>וְאֵין לְאֵל יָדֶךָ</p>
<p class="sf-translit">V'einecha ro'ot v'chalot aleihem kol hayom<br/>v'ein l'el yadecha</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">And your eyes will look, and fail with looking for them, all the day long, and there will be no strength in your hand.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">בַּבֹּקֶר תֹּאמַר מִי־יִתֵּן עֶרֶב<br/>וּבָעֶרֶב תֹּאמַר מִי־יִתֵּן בֹּקֶר</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Baboker tomar mi yiten erev<br/>uva'erev tomar mi yiten boker</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">In the morning you will say, if only it were evening. And in the evening, if only it were morning.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">הַבָּשָׂר עוֹדֶנּוּ בֵּין שִׁנֵּיהֶם<br/>טֶרֶם יִכָּרֵת</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Habasar odenu bein shineihem<br/>terem yikaret</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">The meat was still between their teeth, before it was even chewed.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">בָּכוֹ תִבְכֶּה בַּלַּיְלָה וְדִמְעָתָהּ עַל לֶחֱיָהּ<br/>אֵין־לָהּ מְנַחֵם מִכָּל־אֹהֲבֶיהָ</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Bacho tivkeh balailah v'dim'atah al lechyah<br/>ein lah menachem mikol ohaveiha</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">She weeps bitterly in the night, and her tears are on her cheek. Of all her lovers she has none to comfort her.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">הִנְנִי לֹקֵחַ מִמְּךָ אֶת־מַחְמַד עֵינֶיךָ בְּמַגֵּפָה<br/>וְלֹא תִסְפֹּד וְלֹא תִבְכֶּה וְלוֹא תָבוֹא דִּמְעָתֶךָ</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Hin'ni lokeach mim'cha et machmad einecha b'magefah<br/>v'lo tispod v'lo tivkeh v'lo tavo dim'atecha</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">I am taking from you the delight of your eyes at a single stroke. And you will not mourn, and you will not weep, and your tears will not come.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">וְהָיָה בְלִבִּי כְּאֵשׁ בֹּעֶרֶת עָצֻר בְּעַצְמֹתָי<br/>וְנִלְאֵיתִי כַּלְכֵל וְלֹא אוּכָל</p>
<p class="sf-translit">V'hayah v'libi k'esh bo'eret atzur b'atzmotai<br/>v'nil'eiti calkel v'lo uchal</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">And it was in my heart like a burning fire shut up in my bones. I was worn out holding it in, and I could not.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">וַיִּנָּחֶם יְהוָה כִּי־עָשָׂה אֶת־הָאָדָם בָּאָרֶץ<br/>וַיִּתְעַצֵּב אֶל־לִבּוֹ</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Vayinachem Adonai ki asah et ha'adam ba'aretz<br/>vayit'atzev el libo</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">And he was sorry that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart.</p>
<p class="sf-orig sf-heb" lang="he" dir="rtl">לוּ חָכְמוּ יַשְׂכִּילוּ זֹאת<br/>יָבִינוּ לְאַחֲרִיתָם</p>
<p class="sf-translit">Lu chachmu yaskilu zot<br/>yavinu l'acharitam</p>
<p class="sf-gloss">If only they were wise, and understood this, and could discern what their end would be.</p>
