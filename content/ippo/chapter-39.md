title: "Chapter 39"
---

I am in the room with the tapes. They fall on my arms, stretched out after I have helped Ippo remove them. The whiteness mixes with the dirty grey of the wraps and hangs there over my clothes. I can't move away from here. The memory of the fight is still engraved in my mind.

The cool breeze shifts from outside to inside in one smooth motion, so smooth that you would not notice the air coming in. I try to listen to it. All I can hear is my own line of thinking rising over every other conscious thought.

I swallow. It is hard. The moment I try to force it down a cramp climbs up my throat. There is no moisture in my mouth. It makes me feel like I was the one in the ring with no drop of water on my tongue, 0but I am sitting at my desk.

The dirty tape on my lap fills my chest with a warmth I cannot explain. The kind that engraves every bit of your soul and leaves you with nothing to do but replay the thoughts that put you inside that field. That I can see Ippo's sweat and tears in this strip of cloth makes me feel that way, and I cannot fathom the reason. **The same burn I used to chase when I stared at my own tapes after a win.** **My hands moved on their own back to the tape.**

My eyes look at the tape, at its marks and black spots. My hands feel the imbalance in the knit. The same imbalance you feel when you grab one end of a rubber and pull, leaving the other end slack. I use my fingertips to feel the impurities. All those years touching tape make me capable of knowing exactly what happened.

I can feel the parts that were ripped forcibly. The parts that are not aligned. Yes, I can feel that stretch mark. It is not natural at all, and it was not my fault.

My eyes hover over it. I sigh, lowering the numbness in my chest, trying to get back into the film inside my head. The video skips. Ippo's head snapping back further than it should have. Just like the doctor told me.

The line of thinking makes the warmth disappear. A sharp cold sphere takes its place in my lungs. I am not a smoker. I don't think I caught a cold. The weather feels cool but it is never that cold. **The same cold I used to feel every time I watched my own fight tapes.** **My fingers moved on their own back across the tape.**

I keep playing my fingers across the tape. The tape will tell me the whole story of what Ippo's hand did. You see the wrinkles in some places, and the impurities I keep touching with the tip of my finger collapse against each other, snapping a piece of the tape between them like a sandwich.

These are Ippo's fingers contracting too much.

I pull the wrap loose and tape it across my own hand. I want to feel it on my arm. The pressure rises as my fingers get suffocated between the layers, and it brings back all those hours I spent on my own as a boxer. **The same echo I used to feel when I wrapped my own hands alone in the gym.** **My hand moved on its own and pulled the tape off quickly.**

My hand is shaking. This shake doesn't make any sense. I have not wrapped it that tight. Maybe this is my sign to forget about it and just think of a way to fix Ippo's tempo.

Maybe.
