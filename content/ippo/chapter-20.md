The face.

That face is in the gym. The face that once took what was dear to him, standing in the place that should have been his. The place Itagaki has been working for months and months to be in, in front of Ippo. His legs grow weak, all that leg work catching up to him at once like a dam exploding in front of his eyes. Imai is there, and Ippo is there with him, smiling with him, calm with him, shy with him. Hands behind the back of his head, body language relaxed, feet planted flat on the floor, shoulders loose, eyes soft and calm like a lamb's.

Itagaki steps back. Slowly. Blood draining from his body the moment he thinks to oppose his senses, his feet already making the decision before his head can argue. That ghost is in front of Ippo. Someone has to save Ippo, if not him, then who would be there for Ippo?

He steps forward. Slowly at first, passing by the boys as they are loud, louder than any trio group has the right to be. Their hands are in the sky, synchronized, singing a song that makes Itagaki want to cover his ears.

"Kill him KIIIIL HIM KIIIIL HIM'

Takamura in the middle, the loudest out of them and the tallest, always at the center of it. Aoki and Kimura going east and west, moving like branches of a tree connected to Takamura's whims. Aoki's voice cracks on every third chant, and he doesn't notice or doesn't care. Kimura is half a beat behind, mouthing the words more than singing them, but his body sways in time because Takamura's rhythm is impossible to resist.

The thought of covering his ears took Itagaki's eyes directly to Takamura, who looked like the most cheerful person in the room. But his eyes told a different story. His eyes said: don't do anything stupid. They were singing as if this was actually a match, and Takamura was conducting it, but underneath the noise his gaze was tracking Itagaki's feet, watching where they were headed.

Itagaki looks at Ippo. Ippo is having a conversation with the one person Itagaki wants to put his fist in and break, yet Ippo is charming as usual, looking at a man like that and still being respectful with how he acts. Then he looks at Imai. Imai is looking down at Ippo. No expression at all on his face. Like Ippo is something to be measured, not greeted.

Seeing Imai look at Ippo like that makes Itagaki's steps faster. His chest gets heavy. His blood boils, pressure mounting on top of him, his legs growing heavier with each step. The tips of his fingers can sense the fabric inside his shoes, the free space inside them giving him just enough grip to stand still and not fall on his face. The band on his hand gets looser the more he walks. He starts worrying about it, everything looking horrible as he moves forward and forward. He starts humming. A song he knows, something to steady himself.

Ippo notices him. That soft shy face of his shifts, his eyebrows drop, his mouth parts slightly, something worried crossing his expression. His shoulders lean forward as if he wants to step toward Itagaki, dropping his guard and that gentle look he makes whenever he sees someone new. Ippo is less of a lamb now. More like someone who sees trouble walking his way.

Imai changes his expression. Not much, just enough. His eyes narrow a fraction. Is Ippo ignoring him? Looking away from him? What has he done to him? Then he notices the source. He notices Itagaki, who is walking toward them with his hand on his forehead like someone swatting at a bug, a quick sharp motion, killing something invisible before it flies away.

The band on Itagaki's hand feels like it will fall on his head so he makes a swift move to catch it. But nothing, it doesn't fall. His hand just hangs there in the air, and Imai sees it, and Ippo is looking at Itagaki now.

Itagaki sees Imai's lips move. Just a bit. A twitch at the corner of his mouth, not a smile, something smaller and worse. That is a sign. That is a sign and it sets him on fire.

"Kill him! Kill him! Kill him!" the boys chant behind him.

Itagaki was walking. Now he is not walking. He is moving, a swift stride toward them, closing the distance, and the chanting behind him grows louder. Takamura screams, "Yes, yes, boy! Do it! Do it! Go to jail!" and the three of them start cheering like a squad at a tournament, arms pumping, Aoki nearly tripping over Kimura, the whole corner of the gym turning into a stadium.

Imai looks at them. His face says: what the fuck are they doing? He is completely ignoring Itagaki as he approaches. Looking behind him at the noise, as if the entertainment is back there, not in front of him.

"I'll take the spar," Itagaki says.

Imai is busy looking at the champion. His eyes scan Ippo from feet to shoulders. "Oh," he says. "So that's the champion. I get the fuss now."

"Hey!" Itagaki screams. "Don't ignore me!"

Imai turns his head. Slowly. As if noticing a sound he wasn't sure he heard. "So you are here?" he says. 'I came to spar someone more powerful, not you who's actting like a liltle girl who keeps on trying to show everyone that she is here'

Itagaki tells him to hurry up. His body moves before his words finish, he grabs Imai's shirt and pushes. The fabric stretches in his fist. His arm locks. His legs drive into the ground. He pushes with everything his body has.

Imai doesn't flinch.

He doesn't even look at Itagaki. He keeps looking past him, behind him, at the boys who have gone quiet now, at the gym that has stopped making noise. Itagaki's hand is full of fabric and his arm is shaking and the body behind that shirt will not move. Not a centimeter. Not a fraction. It is like pulling at a boulder. Not just a boulder, a boulder guided by a mountain behind it, rooted into something Itagaki cannot reach.

"You are like a dog," Imai says. He still hasn't moved an inch. "Bark and no bite."

The gym is silent. Takamura's grin has widened but his mouth is closed. Aoki's hands are frozen mid-chant. Kimura's arms are crossed and his jaw is tight. Ippo is between them now, worried, pushing them apart with both hands, telling them that he'll do it instead, he'll take the spar, there's no need for this.

Itagaki closes his eyes.

His hand releases the fabric. He steps aside. "As I said," he mutters, but the sentence has no weight. It falls out of his mouth like something he rehearsed for a moment that already passed.

He stands there. Imai isn't worried. Imai doesn't think Itagaki can stop him. The acknowledgment Itagaki wanted, even as an enemy, even as someone worth fighting, isn't there. His hands unclench. The anger doesn't leave fast, it just goes cold, sitting in his chest like something heavy that stopped burning.

Ippo steps in. The spar is agreed to. Preparations begin. Itagaki is left standing there, his hand still feeling the fabric of a shirt that didn't move. His fingers close around nothing.

Ippo is getting his gloves on. Coach walks over and leans close, his mouth near Ippo's ear, his hand on the kid's shoulder. He whispers something. Itagaki can't hear it. Nobody can hear it.

But he can see the change.

Ippo's eyes lose the lamb. Those are not the eyes of someone shy, someone respectful, someone who wants peace. Those are the eyes of someone excited. Someone ready. Itagaki has seen that look before, right before the first bell, right before the body stops thinking and starts hunting.

Ippo is looking at him.

No. Not at him. Something behind him.

Who might that be?
