---
title: "Chapter 44"
---

I have to search for your name, black haired kid.

If only there was a way to get access to all information without moving away from my bed, such a thing would save me the trouble of staring at this store in front of me.

I feel like I want to yawn as hard as I can because of it. My cheeks expand all across my face as they stretch every bit of intact skin that I have and as I try to stop myself from doing it, it would render my jaws insufferably tense so I can do nothing but surrender to it just like I did surrender to this body and retired.

The wooden door stands in front of my face unlike any other store in this country, none are wooden like that. The red-brownish color coming from it was a pigment I had passed by a couple of times but was never tempted by it. It sits there heavy in its frame like it belongs to a European street, not here, and maybe that is why I had always looked away from it before.

And here I am stuck in my spot on my way to enter it.

Something is poking me at the back of my eyes, no not my eyes but something else, it is straight up needles that press on the back of my neck, tingling that one spot which is why I thought it was my eyes. I extend my arm and scratch it although scratching does nothing at all, my nails just drag across skin that feels normal to the touch while the itch sits deeper, underneath, in a place my fingers can not reach. The same way every sensation I felt in the middle of the stadium was never truly on the surface where you could point to it.

But nonetheless.

I know nothing at all about boxing other than the fact that they obviously have rules, a ring, and you probably can not kill someone during a fight. I mean can you? If it happens by mistake, I think you can, after all you are hitting the face of a human being and that voice I heard in the gym of that kid was like a bullet.

A shiver entered my body at this thought. To see a human being thrown down on a ground that is bouncy and unsteady compared to the street pavement, and for that human to not move at all despite ten seconds passing by, despite everyone trying to wake them up, to then be surprised by someone saying that this boxer is not breathing and his heart has stopped and the weapon was your knuckles. My arms tightened at the elbows and pressed closer to my ribs as if the shiver needed a smaller space to pass through.

---

The store looks from the outside like a small box that makes my eyes want to be closed. And see that darkness that everyone closing their eyes would see, it is not a darkness of blindness but rather the darkness of a veil being sent onto your eyes, reminding me of one time I was a little kid, woke up at night and all I could see was darkness yet my first thought was that I had now turned blind.

But the moment you enter the bookstore, colors hit you like a firecracker.

The spines of the books are everywhere, packed tight in rows that go from the floor to above my head. Red ones next to white ones next to a green so bright it hurts my eyes for a second before they adjust. The deliberate mind that made them in that order, someone who cared enough to place each one by hand, and it makes me want to walk deeper into that maze and take one. So it seems like I will not fight it. I do not have a plan to approach this after all.

Walking by across these books and magazines all alone this time, I look at the pictures on the magazine covers gathering up on the side and my jaw sets itself without me telling it to. A tightness that starts at the teeth and spreads down into the throat where it sits like something I swallowed wrong. I try to clear it, that little cough you do when nothing is actually stuck, and it does nothing. The tightness stays.

The people who are also walking by searching for what they truly want, if they truly know what they want, and meanwhile I try to put my hands into my pockets. This habit that I had gathered up across my childhood that even extended to me doing it on the pitch despite having no pockets in my official football kit. My thumbs press against the seams of the fabric in there, pressing hard enough to feel the thread lines through the skin.

But this does not ease the cold sitting in my chest at all. Each inhale catches halfway as though something blocked the bottom of my lungs and the air has nowhere to land, and it keeps on rising up and up, overcoming every obstacle that my mind would make, for it just reminds me of a day that was the source of my joy. No. It is not.

Let me just get in deeper and search for a random Japanese boxing magazine, as it would seem like I am lost regardless and it never hurts to pick one of them. Especially since I see one with a fighter on the cover whose chest is split open by a scar that runs from the collarbone down past where the frame of the photograph cuts it off. That is unsettling. Maybe this is for a movie? In all cases let's see.

---

As I pick up the magazine my fingers can not help it but walk slowly across its surface, the sensation of touching glorified plastic across the tip of my finger while the weightlessness of the whole thing rests upon my other hand and this makes my fingers grip harder onto it so that it will not fall from my hands on the ground and, ah, I do not want everyone to look at me just like how they did before in a bookstore when I caused a whole section to fall.

This place is surprisingly active, the number of people that walk in and by is substantial, and it makes me unable to truly focus on what I have to search for. The more I flip the pages the more I can not find his face, no matter what Japanese division I search through. I doubt he would weigh a lot so probably in the lower weight categories, but I think the fact that he is tall makes it unlikely for him to be in the lowest ones.

Okay little kid, I did not really call you at all. You were the one who called me and here I am getting no reply, but that is just something that I am used to. My chest is tightening again in that spot just left of center, the same spot that always tightened before a final, and I can feel it taking the space away from my lungs slowly, squeezing harder without me being able to do anything about it.

The whole place and the books that are here make it sharper the more time passes and it pushes deeper into a place I do not think I could touch even if I opened my chest surgically. The covers of the books along the wall catch the store light in small flashes as people walk by and disturb the air, and each flash reminds me of how she used to moonwalk every time I was in a bookshop with her.

The movements that she would do on the ground put a smile on my face. The widening of my teeth that I do not want to show to anyone, but they do widen for her and for that memory.

The ground squeaking by the shoes that rip through it as they slide for a moonwalk. I only feel at ease as they echoed every time in my ears.

My thumb has stopped moving across the magazine page. I did not notice it stop.

As I was holding that same magazine I see a picture of that scary guy with the scar on his chest. He turned out to be a boxing champion of the world in a division. That caught me by surprise as I thought he was just a movie star. I tilt the page under the store light to get a better look but his face sits at an angle where the gloss catches everything and all I get is a blur of jaw and eyebrow and nothing that sticks.

Takamura, ha?

What a weird fellow human being. Although he is interesting, I think I might just pick a random page and take that division of weight to search deeply for.

I am in the Featherweight division now......
